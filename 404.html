What is on the desk

Buyers

A working buyer list you can narrow by city or state, then download.

Seller leads

Purchased files, a live account pull, the public Speed to Seller board, and leads added on the desk stay in separate books.

Maps

Buyers and leads on a street map, in color, so a market is visible before a call.

Deal predictor

A read on the deal from the desk value, plus buyers already in the book who fit that market.

Deal Blaster

Offer sheets built from the lead details, including one file for a whole book. Nothing is emailed until you say which file and say go.

How an offer is figured

The contract offer is 65% of the desk value, rounded to the nearest $500. The price shown to the end buyer adds an assignment fee. That fee starts at $10,000 and can be changed on the deal before a file is downloaded.
